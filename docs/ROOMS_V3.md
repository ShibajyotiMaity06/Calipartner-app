# Rooms and onboarding revision — 24 October 2026

The later [friends and room-limit revision](FRIENDS_AND_ROOM_LIMITS.md) updates Plus to three hosted rooms and adds free username search and limited friend requests. It supersedes the affected limits and Partner Finder statements below.

This owner-requested revision supersedes conflicting room, subscription and onboarding requirements in the PRD, build plan and UI specification. Scope is limited to this revision.

- Free: one hosted room, two people, with neither person required to subscribe. Shared nutrition is limited to calories, protein, fibre and carbohydrates, subject to each person's privacy choices. Logged-today and trained-today status are included. Other members' base tables remain private.
- Plus: two hosted rooms, up to five people in each. Only the host needs a plan to admit people three through five. Members join free.
- Pro: five hosted rooms, retaining the existing 20-person capacity and all existing Pro benefits. Six or more people require a Pro host.
- Sending text chat and sharing an exact workout require the sender to have Plus or Pro. Free recipients can read shared messages and workouts. Sending images or videos requires Pro. Pair challenges require both participants to have Plus or Pro.
- A paid room whose host loses the required subscription freezes immediately. For 30 days an existing member with a sufficient subscription and an available hosted-room slot can take over as host, or the host can renew. After 30 days the room is deleted. Personal diaries and workouts are retained. Ordinary free pair rooms never expire for lack of a subscription.
- Subscription amounts and existing benefits are retained. Plus trials retain seven days; Pro has no trial.
- Pro includes a monthly body and routine review. Ask for current weight and changed details; show recalculated macros for confirmation before saving a new plan. Never silently change targets.
- Height supports centimetres or feet/inches independently of weight units. Ask about water and step goals after goal weight/pace. Make UI moderately smaller using shared tokens while preserving font scaling and 44-point touch targets.
- Highlight selected training days clearly. Remove the home leaf. Before the paywall, show a few short, combined premium benefits without tier names. Tier breakdown belongs on the paywall.
- Canceling an upgrade never continues automatically into room creation. Free pair creation remains available explicitly. Larger squads require the appropriate host plan.
- Remove the numeric weekly-limit warning copy. Calculator and database calorie floors and rate caps remain enforced.

Assumptions: Free hosts one room; Pro retains 20 members; raw shared nutrition is an explicit exception to the earlier percent-only UI rule, with privacy opt-in retained. No new numeric prices were supplied.
